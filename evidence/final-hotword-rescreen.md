## A. Game-momentum evidence

### Steal An Egg

- The official [Roblox experience page](https://www.roblox.com/games/107778070777162/Steal-An-Egg) confirms the game loop: steal eggs, hatch rare pets, earn money, upgrade treadmill/base, gain speed, and pursue rarer eggs/pets/sizes/mutations. Checked 2026-08-25.
- [SpawnScope](https://spawnscope.com/games/steal-an-egg/) says its figures are daily reads from the public Roblox API. Its **last collection on 2026-08-23** reports 720,960 concurrent players, 228,238,374 visits, a 721K period peak, +44.1% in 24 hours, and +286% in 7 days. **Checked 2026-08-25.**
- [RobloxTracker](https://www.robloxtracker.net/games/steal-an-egg-10563114921) shows a conflicting later page snapshot: 536,297 playing, 327.34M visits, and 2.19M all-time peak. **Checked 2026-08-25.** This inconsistency is exactly why later deliverables must give tracker source + snapshot date rather than assert one permanent CCU or visit count.
- The update log on SpawnScope records multiple Roblox timestamp changes in the Aug 16–23 window. **Checked 2026-08-25.**
